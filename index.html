Questions

Keep it simple.

What is Samexxa?

Samexxa is a marketplace concept connecting shoppers, local stores and online sellers across everyday categories.

Can local businesses join?

Yes. The business experience is designed to help local sellers create a digital storefront and reach nearby customers.

What can I discover?

Everyday categories such as grocery, electronics, fashion, home, beauty, books, kids and more.

Is this page the complete marketplace?

This page is the brand and discovery experience. Product catalogue, authentication, checkout, delivery and seller operations can be connected to the backend as the platform grows.
