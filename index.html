Local shopping, simplified

Samexxa is a marketplace built to make everyday shopping easier. Instead of switching between different apps for groceries, gadgets or a birthday outfit, everything sits inside one cart, one checkout and one delivery tracker.

Built in Lucknow, Samexxa connects neighbourhood stores and sellers with the reach they need to grow, and gives shoppers a faster way to find what they're already looking for close to home.
